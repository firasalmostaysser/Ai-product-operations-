# 06 - Job Search OS (Tunisia + Remote)

This is your system to convert effort into interviews.

## 1) Role categories to target

Prioritize these titles in searches:

- AI Product Operations
- Product Operations Specialist
- Revenue Operations Analyst (AI/Automation)
- GTM Operations Specialist
- AI Automation Specialist
- Solutions/Implementation Specialist (AI SaaS)
- Technical BDR/SDR (AI tooling)

---

## 2) Priority geographies

1. Tunisia (onsite/remote)
2. MENA remote-friendly startups
3. Europe remote-first startups (especially UK, FR)
4. US remote roles that accept EMEA hours

Use location filters like:

- "Remote EMEA"
- "Remote Europe"
- "Tunisia + Remote"

---

## 3) Weekly application cadence

Minimum weekly targets:

- 20 tailored applications
- 15 follow-up messages
- 10 networking outreaches
- 3 referral requests

Quality rule: every application must include one customized paragraph.

---

## 4) Job application framework

For each job:

1. Extract core requirements
2. Map your evidence to each requirement
3. Customize CV bullets
4. Write custom intro paragraph
5. Submit + log in `data/job_pipeline.csv`
6. Follow up in 3-5 days

---

## 5) Outreach templates

### A) Recruiter outreach

Subject: Application for [Role] - AI Operations profile

Hi [Name],  
I applied for [Role]. I combine backend engineering, product operations, and AI workflow automation. I have built systems that reduce manual ops time and improve delivery speed.  
If useful, I can share a short case study relevant to your team's workflow.

Best,  
Firas

### B) Founder/operator outreach

Hi [Name],  
I liked your work on [company/project]. I specialize in AI-enabled ops workflows (product + GTM), and I built a [short relevant project].  
Would you be open to a quick chat? I can also share a no-fluff 1-page teardown of an ops workflow improvement for your team.

---

## 6) Follow-up sequence

If no response:

- Day 4: short follow-up
- Day 10: send relevant project link
- Day 18: final polite follow-up

Stop after 3 touches unless they reply.

---

## 7) Interview pipeline management

Every Friday, update pipeline statuses:

- Applied
- Followed up
- Screening
- Interview round 1/2/3
- Offer
- Rejected

Record rejection reasons when available. Turn them into weekly improvement tasks.

---

## 8) Rejection recovery protocol

When rejected:

1. Ask for one concrete feedback point.
2. Update your interview answer bank.
3. Publish one new proof-of-work within 72 hours.
4. Send 3 new applications the same day.

Keep momentum.

---

## 9) Minimum monthly outcomes

- 80+ tailored applications
- 40+ targeted outreach messages
- 12+ interviews
- 1+ paid trial / consulting call

If below target, increase outreach volume and tighten tailoring quality.
