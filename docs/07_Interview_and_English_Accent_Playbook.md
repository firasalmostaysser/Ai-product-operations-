# 07 - Interview and English/Accent Playbook

You do not need a "perfect accent."  
You need clear structure, concise answers, and confident delivery.

---

## 1) The interview communication formula

For almost every question, use:

1. Context (what situation)
2. Action (what you did)
3. Result (measurable outcome)
4. Reflection (what you learned)

This structure matters more than accent.

---

## 2) 30-second self-introduction (memorize)

> I started as a backend engineer and then moved into product ownership, where I led cross-functional delivery and client operations.  
> Over time, I specialized in AI-powered operations by building automation workflows that reduce manual work and speed up execution.  
> Today I am focused on AI Product Operations and GTM Operations roles where I can combine technical depth, product thinking, and measurable business impact.

---

## 3) Accent and clarity strategy (practical)

### Before each interview

- Speak slightly slower than normal.
- Keep sentences short.
- Pause after each important point.
- Avoid long complex grammar.

### During interview

If someone does not understand, use:

> "Let me rephrase that briefly."

Then answer in shorter format.

### Daily speaking drill (15 minutes)

1. Pick one project.
2. Explain it out loud in 90 seconds.
3. Record yourself.
4. Listen once and remove filler words.
5. Repeat once with clearer pauses.

Do this daily for 30 days and your clarity will improve strongly.

---

## 4) Top interview questions + answer skeletons

### Q1: Tell me about yourself

Use the 30-second intro + one relevant project + why this role.

### Q2: Why this role?

1. You enjoy operational problem solving.
2. You use AI to improve execution speed and quality.
3. You want to drive measurable outcomes in a team environment.

### Q3: Describe an automation you built

- Problem: manual bottleneck
- Solution: workflow/agent architecture
- Result: time saved, error reduction, throughput gain

### Q4: How do you work with technical and non-technical teams?

Highlight translation skill:

> "I convert business goals into clear technical tasks, and technical constraints into business decisions."

### Q5: Why should we hire you?

> "I combine technical implementation, product prioritization, and operational execution, and I deliver measurable improvements quickly."

---

## 5) Questions you should ask interviewers

Always ask 3-5 of these:

1. What would success look like in the first 90 days?
2. Which operational bottlenecks are most painful today?
3. How is AI currently used by this team, and where is the biggest gap?
4. Which metrics will this role directly influence?
5. What does excellent cross-functional collaboration look like here?

Strong questions show strategic maturity.

## 7) Confidence scripts (use exactly)

If nervous:

> "I might speak a bit slowly to stay clear and precise. Thank you."

If interrupted:

> "Happy to jump there. I will finish this point in 10 seconds."

If you blank out:

> "Great question. I want to give a precise answer, so I will structure it in two parts."

Professional clarity wins.

---

## 8) Weekly interview training plan

- 2 mock interviews (friend or self-recording)
- 1 behavioral practice session
- 1 technical/product case practice
- Review and refine answer bank

Keep an `interview_answers.md` file and improve it weekly.
