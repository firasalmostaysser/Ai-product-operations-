# 09 - Global Demand and Compensation (Outside Tunisia Focus)

This answers your core question:

> "Is this field needed and well paid globally?"

Short answer: **Yes.**
AI-enabled operations roles are in demand, and compensation is generally stronger in remote-first international companies than local-only markets.

---

## 1) Is this role needed worldwide?

### Strong demand signals

- **World Economic Forum (Future of Jobs 2025)** highlights AI and big data skills among the fastest-growing globally.
- **LinkedIn Work Change Report (2025)** indicates major skill shifts and rapid growth in AI-related hiring.
- Startups and scaleups increasingly need people who can connect:
  - product workflows,
  - GTM execution,
  - AI automation,
  - cross-functional operations.

Your target profile (technical + product + operations + AI execution) matches this demand.

---

## 2) Is it well paid?

Compensation varies by role, level, and company stage. Typical international ranges (snapshot-style guidance, verify per listing):

| Role Family | Typical Range (Europe/Remote) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Product Operations | ~GBP 42k-100k+ | Higher in London and larger tech companies |
| AI Operations / AI Ops Manager | ~EUR 55k-158k | Wide band by scope and seniority |
| RevOps / GTM Ops | ~GBP 45k-110k+ | Manager-level can go significantly higher with equity |
| Technical BDR/SDR (AI SaaS) | Base + variable | Strong upside with commission and promotion path |

Important:
- Entry and mid-level international roles may still pay less than top US roles.
- Remote compensation can be geo-adjusted by employer.
- Total compensation may include bonus/equity.

---

## 3) Best regions for you (outside Tunisia)

Prioritize:

1. **UK + Ireland remote-friendly teams**
2. **France/Benelux startups with remote ops roles**
3. **Germany/Netherlands product and revops ecosystems**
4. **US companies hiring in EMEA time zones**

Use filters:
- "Remote EMEA"
- "Europe Remote"
- "Work from anywhere in Europe/MENA hours"
- "Async first"

---

## 4) How to increase your pay fast

Higher pay comes from proving outcomes, not only titles.

Do these 4 things:

1. Build portfolio projects with quantified impact (time saved, throughput, response speed).
2. Show reusable systems (not one-off demos).
3. Demonstrate communication quality in interviews (clarity > accent perfection).
4. Position yourself as "operator who ships measurable AI workflows."

---

## 5) Salary targeting strategy (practical)

For each role, define:

- **Floor** (minimum acceptable)
- **Target** (realistic goal)
- **Reach** (ambitious)

Track in your notes for each geography and role family.

Never apply without a compensation hypothesis.

---

## 6) Key reality check

The market rewards:

- people who can implement,
- people who can communicate,
- people who can show business impact quickly.

You can do all 3 if you keep executing this system consistently.

---

## 7) Sources to monitor weekly

- World Economic Forum - Future of Jobs
- LinkedIn workforce reports
- Levels.fyi (for role-family benchmarks)
- Talent.com / BuiltIn / region-specific salary pages
- Actual posted salary bands in job descriptions

Treat any single source as directional, not absolute truth.
