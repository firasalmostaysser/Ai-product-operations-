### The Application Strategy

**For each application:**

1. **Research the company first (15 min)**:
   - What does the company do?
   - What stage are they at? (seed/A/B)
   - What AI tools do they use/sell?
   - Any recent news?

2. **Tailor your CV** (use your CV Tailor Agent from Project 1):
   - Rewrite the summary to match the specific role
   - Move the most relevant experience to the top
   - Use their language (copy keywords from job description)

3. **Write a focused cover note (5-7 sentences)**:
   - Open with something specific about them
   - Connect your relevant experience to their specific need
   - One concrete example of relevant work
   - Why you're interested in this company specifically

4. **Follow up strategically**:
   - Find someone at the company on LinkedIn
   - Send a connection request with a short note
   - If they accept, send a brief message (NOT asking for a referral — just showing genuine interest)

5. **Track everything in a spreadsheet**:
   - Company, role, date applied, status, next action
   - Review weekly

### Application Volume Targets

- Weeks 1-3: 3 applications/week (while still building portfolio)
- Weeks 4-8: 5 applications/week
- Month 3+: 8 applications/week (portfolio is strong enough)

**Quality over quantity early on.** 5 tailored applications > 20 generic ones.

## Track B: Freelance AI Automation

### The Fastest Path to First Money

You can get a paid freelance client within 2–4 weeks. Here's exactly how:

**Week 1:** Build Projects 1-3 (simple but impressive)
**Week 2:** Reach out to 10 people in your network
**Week 3:** Offer a free mini-project to one of them
**Week 4:** Turn the free project into a paid project (or get a testimonial)

### Your Network Right Now

Think about everyone you know:
- 50+ startup founders/CTOs you met while selling Softylines HR platform
- Jewelry retailers from Reefq discovery calls
- Legal firms you contacted for the notary tool
- Lions Club connections
- Former colleagues at Softylines
- University alumni

**These people know you.** They've seen your work. They're the easiest first clients.

### The Outreach Script for Network

```
Subject: I'm now building AI automations — thought of you

Hi [Name],

I'm now specializing in AI automation — building systems that automate 
manual workflows using tools like n8n and Claude.

Remember [the thing we worked on or discussed]? I was thinking about that

Happy to do a 30-minute analysis call (completely free) to show you what's

Worth a chat?

Firas
```

### Pricing Your Services

**NEVER undersell.** Low prices attract bad clients and signal low quality.

**Starter pricing (first 3 clients — for testimonials):**
- Discovery call + workflow design: Free (30–45 min)
- Small automation (3–5 nodes, simple flow): $200–$300
- Medium automation (10+ nodes, AI included): $400–$700

**Growth pricing (after 3 testimonials):**
- Small automation: $400–$600
- Medium automation: $800–$1,500
- Complex system (multiple workflows, AI agents): $2,000–$4,000
- Monthly retainer (monitoring + improvements): $400–$800/month

**Project types to pitch:**
1. Email triage and response automation
2. Client reporting automation (weekly/monthly reports auto-generated)
3. CRM enrichment (automatically research leads)
4. Document processing (like your notary tool)
5. Internal knowledge base + AI chatbot
6. Lead qualification automation
7. Social media monitoring and alerts

### Platforms for Finding Freelance Clients

**Warm (best for starting):**
- Your personal network (LinkedIn messages, WhatsApp to former contacts)
- LinkedIn posts that showcase your work (inbound)

**Cold (works with volume):**
- [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com) — largest freelance marketplace. Hard to start but powerful once established.
- [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com) — premium freelance (harder to get in but higher rates)
- [Contra](https://contra.com) — modern freelance platform, no fees
- [Malt](https://www.malt.fr) — strong in France/Europe (your French is an advantage!)

**Community-based:**
- n8n community — people ask for help with workflows all the time
- r/n8n — answer questions, offer to build the solution
- Indie Hackers — founders looking for technical help

### The Upwork Strategy (If You Go This Route)

Upwork is competitive but worth it. Here's how to win:

1. **Choose a specific niche**: "n8n AI Automation for SaaS Startups" — not "automation"
2. **First 3 jobs: accept low rates** ($15–25/hr) to get reviews. After 3 five-star reviews, raise to $50+/hr
3. **Write proposals that show you understand their problem**: Most proposals say "I can do this." Yours should say "I understand your problem. Here's my exact approach. Here's proof."
4. **Add a custom Loom video** to your proposal — almost nobody does this. Instant differentiation.

## The Most Important Mindset Rule

**You are selling the outcome, not the tool.**

Bad: "I build n8n workflows with Claude integration"
Good: "I cut your weekly reporting time from 4 hours to 15 minutes"

Bad: "I have experience with AI automation"
Good: "I helped a notary firm reduce document processing time by 90%"

Every client, every recruiter, every stakeholder cares about: What will change if I hire you? Always answer that question first.
