# CV Templates -- Role-Specific Variations

**One CV does not fit all. Here are templates optimized for each target role.**

---

## General Principles

1. **Tailor every application**. Change the summary, reorder experience, highlight relevant skills.
2. **Lead with impact**, not responsibilities. "Reduced onboarding time by 60%" not "Managed onboarding process."
3. **Include AI tools by name**. Recruiters ctrl+F for them.
4. **Keep it to 1-2 pages**. Respect their time.
5. **Use numbers everywhere**. 80K users, 40+ calls, 5x improvement, 15+ features.

## Tips for Each Application

1. **Read the job description 3 times**
2. **Highlight keywords** they use
3. **Mirror their language** in your CV
4. **Map your experience** to their requirements (1:1)
5. **Add a "Why [Company]" line** in your cover letter
6. **Always include portfolio links**
