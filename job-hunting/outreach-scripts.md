### Post Type 2: Share a Lesson

```
I lost a job opportunity because of a language barrier.

Not my language skills -- I speak three languages fluently 
(Arabic, French, English).

The issue? Accent clarity on a video call.

So I did what I do with any problem: I automated my improvement.

- Built a daily speaking practice routine using ChatGPT Voice
- Used AI pronunciation coaching (Elsa Speak)
- Shadow-practiced with YouTube videos of product leaders

30 days later, my clarity improved dramatically.

The lesson: When you face a barrier, don't just feel bad about it. 
Build a system to overcome it.

#CareerGrowth #AITools #BuildInPublic
```

### Post Type 3: Share Industry Insight

```
I analyzed 50 AI job postings this week. Here's what I found:

The #1 skill companies want in AI Product Ops isn't coding.

It's the ability to USE AI tools to solve operational problems.

Specifically:
- 82% mention "AI tools" or "AI-powered workflows"
- 64% mention "automation" (n8n, Make, Zapier)
- 56% mention "cross-functional coordination"
- Only 28% require coding as a primary skill

The takeaway: If you can USE AI to make operations faster 
and better, you're more valuable than someone who can 
BUILD AI models but can't apply them.

#AIJobs #ProductOperations #CareerAdvice
```
