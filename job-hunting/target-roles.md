## Your Top 6 Target Roles

### 1. AI Product Operations

**What it is**: Own day-to-day operations of AI products. Bridge between engineering, product, and customers.

**Why you fit**: Product Owner experience + technical background + AI automation skills.

**Salary range**: $40K-80K (remote EMEA), $80K-150K (US remote)

**Where to find**:
- LinkedIn: Search "AI Product Operations"
- AngelList/Wellfound: AI startup roles
- Y Combinator's Work at a Startup: workatastartup.com
- Company career pages: Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, Vercel

**Companies hiring for this**:
- AI product companies (Storyteller, Jasper, Copy.ai, Writer)
- AI-powered SaaS (Notion, Linear, Figma -- they all have AI features now)
- AI agencies and studios

### 2. AI Solutions Engineer

**What it is**: Help customers implement and succeed with AI products. Technical + customer-facing.

**Why you fit**: You can code AND talk to clients. That's rare.

**Salary range**: $50K-90K (EMEA), $100K-180K (US)

**Where to find**:
- LinkedIn: "Solutions Engineer AI"
- Company career pages
- Networking in AI communities

**Companies to target**:
- Anthropic, OpenAI, Cohere (AI model providers)
- LangChain, n8n, Make (AI tooling companies)
- Supabase, Vercel, Railway (developer platforms with AI features)

### 3. GTM / Revenue Operations (AI)

**What it is**: Strategy and operations for bringing AI products to market.

**Why you fit**: Product thinking + market awareness + technical credibility.

**Salary range**: $45K-75K (EMEA), $80K-130K (US)

**Where to find**:
- LinkedIn: "GTM Operations" + "AI"
- RevOps community job boards
- SaaS job boards

### 4. AI Automation Consultant / Builder

**What it is**: Help businesses automate processes using AI + tools like n8n, Make, etc.

**Why you fit**: Technical ability + client discovery experience + AI tools mastery.

**Income potential**: $2K-10K/month freelance, scales with clients.

**Where to find clients**:
- Upwork: Search for "automation" or "n8n" projects
- Fiverr: AI automation services
- Direct outreach: Contact businesses that need automation
- n8n community: People looking for builders
- LinkedIn: Post about automations, attract inbound leads

### 5. Technical BDR/SDR (AI Products)

**What it is**: Business development focused on developer tools and AI products.

**Why you fit**: Technical credibility + sales instincts + community building.

**Salary range**: $35K-60K base + commission (EMEA)

**Companies to target**:
- Supabase, Vercel, Netlify, Railway
- Cursor, Replit, GitHub
- n8n, Make, Zapier
- AI API companies

### 6. AI Implementation Specialist

**What it is**: Help companies adopt and integrate AI into their operations.

**Why you fit**: Technical + product + client discovery. Perfect triad.

**Salary range**: $45K-80K (EMEA)

**Where to find**:
- Consulting firms adding AI practices
- AI-focused agencies
- Enterprise software companies adding AI features

## Networking Strategy

### Warm Outreach (Higher Success)

1. **Alumni**: Search for Polytechnique de Sousse graduates at target companies
2. **Community connections**: People you've interacted with in Discord/Twitter
3. **Former colleagues**: Anyone from Softylines network
4. **Lions Club**: Professional contacts from your community work

### Cold Outreach (Volume)

Target: 10 cold outreach messages per week

**Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific thing about their role/company/post]. 

I'm building AI automation tools and transitioning into 
[AI Product Ops/GTM] -- your work at [Company] on [specific project] 
is exactly the kind of thing I want to do.

Would you be open to a 15-minute chat? I'd love to hear about 
your experience with [specific topic].

Thanks,
Firas
```
