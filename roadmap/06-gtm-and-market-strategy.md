## What GTM Means in AI

Go-to-market is how a company gets its product to customers. In AI, this is especially complex because:

1. **The market is new** -- Buyers don't always know what they need
2. **Products change fast** -- What you sell today may be different in 3 months
3. **Education is part of selling** -- You often have to teach before you can sell
4. **Trust is hard to build** -- AI has a credibility problem ("does it actually work?")

## The AI GTM Framework

### Step 1: Market Understanding

**Who buys AI products?**

| Buyer Type | What They Want | How They Buy |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Enterprise (1000+ employees) | Reliability, security, compliance | Long sales cycle, procurement, pilots |
| Mid-Market (100-1000) | ROI, integration, support | Medium cycle, champion-driven |
| SMB (< 100) | Speed, simplicity, price | Self-serve, quick decisions |
| Developer | Great docs, free tier, community | PLG, bottom-up adoption |
| Non-Technical | No-code, outcomes, hand-holding | Demo-driven, relationship sales |

**Your sweet spot**: Mid-market and SMB companies that need AI but don't have AI expertise in-house. You bridge that gap.

### Step 2: Positioning

**Positioning Framework (memorize this):**

```
FOR [target customer]
WHO [has this problem]
[Product name] IS A [category]
THAT [key benefit]
UNLIKE [alternatives]
WE [key differentiator]
```

**Example for an AI automation service:**

```
FOR small and mid-size businesses in EMEA
WHO waste 20+ hours per week on manual data processing
AI Workflow Solutions IS AN automation consulting service
THAT eliminates repetitive work using AI-powered workflows
UNLIKE generic IT consultants
WE specialize in AI automation with n8n and Claude, 
delivering 10x productivity improvements in weeks, not months.
```

### Step 3: Channels

**Where to reach AI product buyers:**

| Channel | Best For | Your Action |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| LinkedIn | B2B, thought leadership | Post 3x/week about AI automation |
| Twitter/X | Developer community, AI builders | Share builds, engage with AI community |
| Product Hunt | Launching tools | Launch your portfolio projects |
| Communities | Direct engagement | Active in n8n, Cursor, AI Discord |
| Content Marketing | SEO, education | Write guides, tutorials |
| Cold Outreach | Direct sales | Email/LinkedIn DM to prospects |
| Events/Webinars | Credibility, networking | Attend and speak at AI events |
| Partnerships | Distribution | Partner with agencies, consultants |

### Step 4: Sales Motion

**Types of sales motions in AI:**

**1. Product-Led Growth (PLG)**
- Users try the product for free
- Value is self-evident
- Upgrade to paid for more features
- Examples: Cursor, n8n, Supabase

**2. Sales-Led Growth (SLG)**
- Sales team reaches out to prospects
- Demo → Trial → Contract
- Higher ACV (Annual Contract Value)
- Examples: Enterprise AI platforms

**3. Community-Led Growth (CLG)**
- Build community around expertise
- Community members become customers
- Word-of-mouth drives growth
- Examples: Open-source AI tools

**For you**: Start with PLG + CLG. Build tools people can try, build community around your content.

## Competitive Analysis

### How to Analyze AI Competitors

**Framework: SCAMPER Analysis**

For any AI product/competitor, analyze:
- **S**ubstitutes: What else could customers use?
- **C**ombine: What integrations matter?
- **A**dapt: How do they adapt to different markets?
- **M**odify: What recent changes have they made?
- **P**ut to other use: What unexpected use cases exist?
- **E**liminate: What's missing from their product?
- **R**everse: What's their weakness?

## Market Sizing

### TAM, SAM, SOM

- **TAM** (Total Addressable Market): Everyone who could theoretically use AI automation
- **SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market): Businesses in your region/niche
- **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Businesses you can realistically reach and serve

**Example for AI Automation Consulting in EMEA:**

```
TAM: 2M+ businesses in EMEA that could use AI automation
     → $50B+ market

SAM: 100K SMBs in North Africa/Francophone EMEA 
     that need automation, have budget
     → $500M market

SOM: 50-200 businesses you can personally serve
     through outreach, referrals, content
     → $500K-2M revenue potential (your first 2 years)
```

## Pricing Strategy for AI Products

### Common AI Pricing Models

| Model | How It Works | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Usage-based | Pay per API call/token | Developer tools (OpenAI) |
| Seat-based | Pay per user/month | Team tools (Cursor) |
| Tier-based | Feature tiers (Free/Pro/Enterprise) | SaaS products |
| Value-based | Price based on ROI delivered | Consulting, enterprise |
| Hybrid | Seats + usage | Complex products |

### How to Price Your Services

**The Value Pricing Formula:**

1. How many hours does the manual process take? → X hours/month
2. What's the hourly cost of the person doing it? → $Y/hour
3. Monthly cost of manual process = X * Y
4. Your automation saves Z% of that time
5. Monthly value of automation = X * Y * Z%
6. Price your automation at 20-30% of annual value

**Example:**
- Manual process: 40 hours/month at $25/hour = $1,000/month = $12,000/year
- Your automation saves 80% = $9,600/year in value
- Price: $2,000-3,000 one-time + $200-400/month maintenance
